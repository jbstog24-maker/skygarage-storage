What Is the Storage Elevator?

The Storage Elevator is a retractable ceiling lift platform for your garage. Load it, raise it to the ceiling, and your floor is clear again.

Retractable ceiling lift

The platform lowers to you for loading, then lifts your bins and boxes up to the ceiling and out of the way.

Drill powered

The lift is powered by a drill. There is no electrical work needed and no motor to wire in.

Made in Oregon, USA

CeilingSAM Storage Elevator systems are made in Oregon, in the United States.

Common Questions

Straight answers. If your question is not here, ask us when you book.

What ceiling height do I need?

The standard units fit garage ceilings from 8 to 11 feet, and taller versions fit 11.5 to 14 feet. Tell us your ceiling height when you book and we will match the right version to your garage.

How much weight can it hold?

The Storage Elevator holds up to 400 lb, evenly distributed. The Storage Elevator Pro holds up to 700 lb. See the FAQ page for the difference between the two.

Can I park my car under it?

Yes. That is the point of the system. The platform stores your items up at the ceiling so the floor space below stays clear for your car. We confirm the fit for your garage and your vehicle at booking.

How does booking work?

Fill out the booking form with your product choice and a preferred date and time window. We confirm your booking, your unit ships free directly to your home, and an independent installer comes out to install it.

How long does installation take?

Installs take about 1 to 2 hours.

Who installs my Storage Elevator?

Your install is done by an independent installer who carries their own liability insurance. SkyGarage Storage coordinates and schedules your install. The owner does not install himself.
