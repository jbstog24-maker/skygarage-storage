Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about the units, ordering, and installation. If your question is not here, ask us when you book.

What is the difference between the Storage Elevator and the Storage Elevator Pro?

Both are the same 4x8 platform, both lower to you with a cordless drill, and both hold about 16 standard 27 gallon totes. The difference is strength. The Storage Elevator is rated for 400 lb of evenly distributed weight. The Pro is the heavier duty flagship model, rated for 700 lb. If you store heavy gear, tools, or a home shop's worth of equipment, pick the Pro. For typical household totes and seasonal items, the standard Elevator is plenty.

How much weight can it hold?

The Storage Elevator holds up to 400 lb, evenly distributed across the platform. The Storage Elevator Pro holds up to 700 lb, evenly distributed. The platform mounts into your ceiling joists, and we confirm your ceiling is a good fit when you book.

How many storage totes fit on it?

About 16 standard 27 gallon totes on either 4x8 model.

What ceiling height do I need?

The standard units fit garage ceilings from 8 to 11 feet. Taller versions are available for ceilings from 11.5 to 14 feet. Tell us your ceiling height when you book and we will match the right version to your garage.

Does it need a motor or electrical work?

No. The platform is drill powered. You raise and lower it with a standard cordless drill, so there is no motor, no wiring, and no outlet needed.

Can I park my car under it?

Yes. That is the point of the system. Your storage rides up at the ceiling so the floor below stays clear for your car.

How does ordering work?

Order and pay online from the pricing page, or book through the booking form if you want to talk first. Your unit is ordered as soon as your payment clears, and it ships free directly to your home. Once it arrives, we schedule your install.

How long does installation take?

About 1 to 2 hours.

Who installs my Storage Elevator?

An independent installer who carries their own liability insurance. SkyGarage Storage coordinates and schedules your install. The owner does not install himself.

What warranty does it carry?

Every unit is backed by the manufacturer warranty. The units are made in Oregon, USA, from American steel.
